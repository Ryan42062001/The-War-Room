## 2. Decision precedence and provenance contract

**ACCEPTED AND CURRENT:** establish valid canonical availability, own/taken and roster cap before offering a player; off-board picks advance numbered ledger but never acquire invented ECR; compare FantasyPros ECR-derived value first, then existing bounded roster/FLEX/VORP(rank proxy)/scarcity/tier/phase and timing/turn adjustments. ESPN board/ADP and labeled FantasyPros ADP fallback are market inputs only. Need affects choice but is not an unconditional position mandate; QB/TE early timing cannot arbitrarily supersede extreme ECR. The actual snake next pick and count of opponents must underlie now/wait explanations. Adjacent own pick with zero opponents is a **conditional deterministic availability fact**; nonzero-intervening survival is speculative. Same normalized canonical draft state should lead to coherent stable behavior, but new fixture scenarios still require tests rather than assumed winners.

**PROPOSED / NOT APPROVED / NOT IMPLEMENTED — bounded explanation policy:** an actionable card should trace: (1) named candidate, FantasyPros PPR ECR rank/source and tier or genuine no-ECR noncandidate; (2) filled/open dedicated vs FLEX need, rank-proxy VORP or actual tier/scarcity where material; (3) why draft now vs wait: exact own next pick, intervening opponent count, qualitative market signal and explicit ESPN board/ADP vs FantasyPros ADP fallback vs UNKNOWN; (4) limitations: ESPN refresh timestamp only when attributable, missing/stale/per-player unverified timing, manual or unresolved pick state, no calibrated survival or confidence probability. Reuse existing expanded details/metadata; no new engine or second source of truth. Proposed text: 'Decision strength: HIGH (heuristic)', not '83% confidence'; 'Market timing unknown — no survival estimate', not unknown-market '50% survival'; for normal opponent windows 'Market timing suggests earlier/later selection; not a measured probability'; for a *valid* adjacent own turn 'No opponent picks between your two selections'. If only a summary refresh timestamp exists, say 'per-player freshness not verified', not 'this player's ESPN ADP refreshed at T0'. Do not imply structured Direct is independently live validated.

**NEEDS_MANAGER_DECISION / NOT IMPLEMENTED:** whether to withhold actionable DRAFT NOW during conflicting/unresolved ownership or invalid next pick, whether old ESPN fields are discarded or merely warned, what constitutes stale (no invented time cutoff), whether a full own roster with incomplete ledger requires a separate hold, handling unsupported non-PPR/superflex leagues, a new tie-break/winner, QB/TE adjustments or any threshold/weight modification. No new winning player, threshold, weight or policy is approved by this report. Preserve existing current scores, guardrails and labels as internal heuristics until separate decision. Unknown inputs may be represented as unknown, but a neutral numeric model sentinel is NOT proof of 50% occurrence probability.

**UNKNOWN REQUIRING EVIDENCE:** per-player timestamp/rights/provenance for ESPN market, live Companion-to-app source accuracy, representative observed survival or recommendation-confidence calibration, arbitrary custom scoring settings, exact outcomes of new cases below. No provider contact, new dataset, live account or protected model experimentation is allowed to resolve them here.
