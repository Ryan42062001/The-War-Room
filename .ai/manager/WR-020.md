### Manager methodological findings
These do not invalidate the conservative `MORE EVIDENCE NEEDED` result, but they further block promotion:
1. The historical test cohort requires at least four target-season games, so evaluation conditions on players surviving/playing enough in the target season. This measures conditional performance more than complete preseason draft value and omits zero/low-availability outcomes.
2. The paired bootstrap resamples player-season rows independently even though some players recur across held-out seasons. A successor should use player-clustered and season-level uncertainty reporting.
3. Rookies are absent, and age/experience, draft capital, team movement, preseason role, and defensible availability context are missing.
4. No lawful historical FantasyPros comparator was used, so WR-018 cannot establish superiority to the current production authority.
5. The 2022–2025 holdouts have now been observed by the project. A successor may reuse them only as confirmatory evidence with this limitation disclosed; model/feature selection should be frozen using earlier development seasons, while 2026 remains the clean prospective test.
