## Frozen returning-player v1 specification

### Cohort boundary
- Applies to returning QB/RB/WR/TE only.
- Rookies remain separate and are not fitted into this returner model.
- Historical cohort/target construction remains the WR-025 contract unless a later Manager task explicitly revises it.

### Expected-performance ordering
Use the exact WR-025 preseason-known feature matrix and preprocessing.

Feature families include the frozen WR-025 Y-1/Y-2 completed-stat and immutable-metadata inputs: prior PPR/game and games, per-game passing/rushing/receiving opportunity and production, available admitted EPA/share/WOPR fields, safe efficiency ratios, two-year/trend/recency features, age, experience, immutable draft capital, and explicit missing-history indicators.

Model, by position:
`StandardScaler -> Ridge(alpha=100.0)`

Target: active returning-player next-season Full-PPR points/game.

Training/evaluation remains chronological; no target season contributes outcomes to its own fitted parameters.

### Risk / warning architecture
Retain the WR-027 position-specific warning models:
`StandardScaler -> LogisticRegression(C=0.25, class_weight='balanced', solver='liblinear', max_iter=2000)`

Labels remain the accepted WR-027/WR-025 expectation-relative breakout, downside, and low-availability definitions.

Probability handling remains WR-027:
- position/label-specific prior-only recalibration;
- fit a Platt recalibrator only when at least 50 eligible earlier OOS observations with both classes exist;
- otherwise use raw probabilities and explicitly flag the fallback;
- warning tiers use earlier OOS position/label distributions at median / 75th / 90th percentiles;
- if insufficient prior history exists, use the documented `.25/.50/.75` tier fallback and flag it.

Risk outputs are warning/explanation-only. They do not alter returning-player rank.
