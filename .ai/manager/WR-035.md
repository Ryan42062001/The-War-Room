## Objective
Research and freeze, or explicitly decline to freeze, a defensible returning-player **season-total fantasy projection/distribution interface** by composing:
- frozen WR-033 expected active-game PPR/game; and
- accepted WR-034 recorded-game availability/continuation expectation and uncertainty.

The task must determine whether the simple independence approximation

`expected_season_ppr = expected_ppr_per_active_game * expected_games`

is sufficiently accurate/calibrated for downstream use, or whether a predeclared dependence-aware composition materially improves the result.

Do not retune either upstream model. Do not perform Phase-6 replacement/FLEX/MSV/value work. Do not change production rankings.

## Why needed
Phase 6 requires an accepted season-level projection/distribution contract before league replacement and cross-position intrinsic value can be evaluated honestly. WR-033 and WR-034 intentionally separate active-game performance from recorded-game continuation, but `E[performance * games]` need not equal `E[performance] * E[games]` when role/performance and availability are dependent.

WR-035 must validate the composition rather than assume independence.

## Required research questions
### 1. Central season-total expectation
At minimum evaluate the transparent independence product:

`INDEPENDENT_PRODUCT = WR033_expected_ppr_per_active_game * WR034_expected_games`

Compare it against cutoff-safe transparent baselines such as:
- prior-season realized fantasy total adjusted only by a predeclared schedule rule if used;
- frozen WR-033 expected PPR/game multiplied by WR-034 PREV_RATE fallback;
- training-only position mean season total where useful as a sanity baseline.

### 2. Dependence-aware composition
Predeclare at least one transparent dependence-aware alternative if the historical inputs support one without changing WR-033 or WR-034. Examples may include:
- jointly resampling/calibrating historical performance and games residuals by position;
- empirical correlated residual draws;
- a fixed conditional correction learned only from earlier folds.

Do not use a dependence-aware method merely because it is more complex. It must earn adoption under frozen chronological gates.

### 3. Distribution / uncertainty
Produce and evaluate a season-total uncertainty representation suitable for Phase 6. Preferred output is deterministic draws or calibrated quantiles that preserve observed dependence when supported.

At minimum report:
- central expected season points;
- interval/quantile coverage;
- interval width/sharpness;
- tail behavior;
- rank sensitivity within position when meaningful;
- provenance and fallback status.

If only a simple residual interval is defensible, say so explicitly rather than fabricating a precise joint distribution.

### 4. High-value-player applicability
Because WR-034's incremental expected-games lift was smaller in the highest prior-PPR/game quartile and approximately flat versus PREV_RATE for high-tier WR, freeze a descriptive draft-relevant applicability analysis before target evaluation.

This analysis may not retroactively change candidate selection unless its decision rule is predeclared before scoring.

## Evaluation requirements
Use chronological fake-preseason replay. Development/selection must use earlier seasons only; later confirmation cannot rescue a development failure.

Report pooled and by QB/RB/WR/TE, plus by target season where sample permits.

### Central season-total metrics
- MAE;
- RMSE;
- signed bias;
- calibration by predicted-value range;
- position rank MAE / Spearman where meaningful;
- catastrophic over/under-prediction rates under thresholds frozen on earlier folds.

### Distribution metrics
- empirical interval coverage at frozen nominal level(s);
- interval width/sharpness;
- coverage by position, target season, coverage/fallback status, and value tier;
- proper scoring metric such as CRPS or log score if a full predictive distribution is honestly available;
- rank-interval coverage/width if deterministic draws are produced.

### Composition comparison
Directly compare:
- independence product versus transparent baselines;
- selected dependence-aware candidate versus independence product;
- fallback composition versus primary composition.

Use repeated-player-aware uncertainty for the primary candidate differences.

### Robustness
- season-to-season stability;
- source/coverage missingness;
- sensitivity to accepted WR-034 fallback paths;
- deterministic reproducibility;
- extreme zero-game and near-full-season cases;
- high-value-player quartile/decile diagnostics;
- failure-closed behavior if upstream required artifacts/hashes do not match.
