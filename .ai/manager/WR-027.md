## Objective
Determine whether historical downside/breakout/availability signals should modify custom player rankings on a position-specific basis or remain informational warnings, while preserving the successful WR-025 mean-projection model as the primary benchmark.

## Why needed
WR-025 found a strong returning-player mean-projection signal but a universal risk overlay worsened WR and TE rank MAE by more than 5%. Downside classifiers were nevertheless informative, especially at RB/WR/TE. The next uncertainty is calibration and use, not whether all risk should be applied with one global penalty.

- universal risk overlay improved QB/RB rank MAE but regressed WR +5.51% and TE +6.20%;
- rookie Ridge remained worse than transparent rookie prior.

## Required study design

### 1. Preserve the mean benchmark
Treat WR-025 position-specific Ridge expected-PPR/game model as the reference mean-projection architecture.

Do not tune away WR-025's negative risk-overlay result after seeing it.

### 2. Position-specific warning calibration
For QB/RB/WR/TE separately, evaluate downside, breakout, and low-availability probabilities with chronological training only.

Report at minimum where sample sizes permit:
- ROC AUC;
- PR AUC for imbalanced labels;
- Brier score;
- log loss;
- calibration/reliability by probability bucket;
- observed event rate by warning tier;
- stability across scored seasons.

Warnings must use position-specific calibration if evidence supports it; do not force one global threshold.

### 3. Ranking-use question
Compare at least:
A. mean projection only;
B. mean projection + warnings displayed separately with NO rank penalty;
C. predeclared position-specific risk-adjusted ranking rule(s).

Any rank modifier must be learned/frozen using only earlier chronological data for each scored season. No post-hoc coefficient choice on the scored target season.

Primary decision: for each position classify risk use as one of:
- `RANK MODIFIER SUPPORTED`
- `WARNING-ONLY SUPPORTED`
- `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`

### 4. Robust mean-projection challenger
Investigate a robust regression approach intended to reduce occasional Ridge outliers while preserving rank signal. Suitable transparent candidates include Huber-style regression or another justified robust linear method.

Do not replace Ridge merely because RMSE improves.

A robust challenger should report MAE, RMSE, Spearman, position rank MAE, and tail absolute-error behavior versus Ridge.

### 5. Rookie boundary
Keep rookies separate. The richer rookie Ridge model remains unvalidated.

You may analyze rookie warning signals descriptively, but do not combine rookies into the returning-player calibration or claim a validated rookie ranking model.

## Predeclared adoption guard
A position-specific rank modifier is not supported unless it improves or preserves rank quality without materially harming projection quality.

At minimum require, by position over the evaluated historical folds:
- mean position rank MAE improvement >= 2%, OR a statistically/operationally equivalent predeclared improvement criterion justified before scoring;
- no >2% worsening in PPR/game MAE versus the mean-only benchmark;
- Spearman no worse by >0.01;
- no recurring severe season-level regression hidden by pooled averages.

If warning probabilities calibrate usefully but the rank modifier fails this guard, classify that position `WARNING-ONLY SUPPORTED`.
