## 2. Purely symbolic per-position support and precision (no actual power result)

Let `p∈{QB,RB,WR,TE}`, `Y∈{2027,2028}`; `N_reg(p,Y)` is the hypothetical number of **preseason registered** eligible returning IDs; `K_obs(p,Y)` is the number of unique player clusters with a **valid, predeclared** target rate; `M_zero` and `M_missing` are registered zero-game/undefined-rate and missing/invalid target groups. A named independent statistical reviewer must **later**, under lawful pre-outcome source/cohort access, assess whether each position has adequate effective independent clusters and power **before target observation**; current WR-114 uses no observed or estimated counts.

For a hypothetical rate-evaluable player `i`, set `d_i=|candidate_i−target_i|−|persistence_i−target_i|` (negative = improved MAE); each model and baseline must score **identical predeclared matched rows**. With future position mean baseline absolute error `B(p,Y)>0`, define `R(p,Y)=mean(d_i)/B(p,Y)`. A separately chosen, independently justified **future** per-position noninferiority margin `m_p` would require a simultaneous upper confidence limit `U_p(R(p,2028))≤m_p` for **each** QB/RB/WR/TE, along with predeclared positive pooled benefit and season/tail/rank/source-lineage gates. This is an illustrative **form** of a possible future guard, not adoption of a threshold or of the old failed v2.1 RB `0.05` limit. The eventual audited protocol must determine hypothesis direction, paired ratio denominator/zero behavior, eligible weights, resampling method, tail metrics, multiplicity/error budget, stopping and evidence to claim benefit. Per-position noninferiority alone cannot prove improvement.

**Precision illustration only:** For invented independent player-cluster paired-loss dispersion `s_p`, hypothetical one-sided error allocation `α_p` and effective independent support `K_eff,p`, a normal-type half-width is proportional to `z_(1−α_p)s_p/√K_eff,p`. A theoretical sample planning relation `K_needed,p ≳ [(z_(1−α_p)+z_(1−β_p))s_p/δ_p]^2` would depend on *pre-outcome assumed* detectable effect `δ_p>0`, desired power `1−β_p` and correctly modeled dependence. These are **symbols only**, not estimates from NFL data. As a wholly **fabricated illustration**, if two imaginary cohorts each have identical paired-loss dispersion and one has four times the genuinely independent clusters, its idealized standard error would be one-half as large. This cannot provide a real position count, candidate success probability or minimum executable number.

**Dependence/attrition:** If a future protocol creates repeated rows or unequal player weights, a descriptive `K_eff=(Σw_i)^2/Σ(w_i²)` detects weight concentration **only under its assumptions**; it does not remove team/opponent/game correlation or create independent season-level observations. Across 2027/2028, repeat player identities must be linked in any paired or joint resampling; shared year conditions invalidate treating hundreds of player rows as hundreds of independent **seasons**. Predeclare all `N_reg/K_obs/M_zero/M_missing` by position/year, forecast fallback/abstention, trades/team/position changes, prior-year absent baseline, full-PPR numerator/recorded-game denominator, ID failure and source correction. Target-year zero games → **undefined rate**, not zero-points-per-game. Report *both* complete preseason population/availability and conditional-on-playing rate subset; do not exclude low-performing players after observing outcomes or claim a pure rate result describes games availability. If informative dropout or target missingness invalidates one position's intended inference, `INSUFFICIENT` despite a good pooled score.

**Simultaneous four-position and year-sensitive gate:** A future independent statistician specifies prospective familywise error budget/correction or validated simultaneous cluster intervals across **all four** position safety claims and any multiple arms/baselines/seasons, plus separately predeclared pooled superiority. All position-specific uncertainty must be interpretable after missingness, ratio-baseline variance and heavy tails. One 2028 confirmation season is a **single** temporal test; within-year player bootstrap cannot estimate between-confirmation-season regime variability.
