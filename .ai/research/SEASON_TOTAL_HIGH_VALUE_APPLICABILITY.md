# WR-035 High-Value-Player Applicability

The independence product materially beat both transparent baselines among preseason-known high-value cohorts, but absolute uncertainty remained large.

| Confirmation cohort | N | Independence MAE | Paired MAE | WR033 × PREV_RATE MAE | Prior-total MAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Prior-PPR/game Q4 | 449 | 69.991 | 70.037 | 81.138 | 93.877 |
| Prior-PPR/game D10 | 184 | 90.298 | 89.065 | 100.533 | 117.095 |
| WR Q4 | 172 | 71.749 | 72.375 | 78.065 | 94.068 |

The paired distribution's nominal 80% interval covered only 61.0% of Q4, 50.0% of D10, and 64.0% of WR Q4 outcomes. Accordingly, pooled distribution calibration is supported, but high-value-player intervals are not sufficiently calibrated for confident tail claims. Phase 6 may use the central expectation for high-value players with explicit low-confidence/rank-sensitivity flags; it should not treat the reported high-value p10/p90 bounds as calibrated guarantees.
