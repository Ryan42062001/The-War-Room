## 3. Hypothetical, pre-result statistical adequacy — no actual player counts or power estimates

The independent statistician/Auditor in a **later, separately authorized protocol-design gate** would determine achievable precision and required effective sample **for every** `p ∈ {QB, RB, WR, TE}` and both `Y ∈ {2027, 2028}` from a lawful source/cohort-information process. This report performs **no** real-player count, variance estimate, precision estimate, hyperparameter search or executable margin/threshold freeze; publicly known historical WR-101 aggregates cannot choose a new RB margin or infer prospective sampling power.

**Paired estimand and baselines.** For a hypothetical registered returning player `i` with valid target rate `y_(i,p,Y)`, let `a_i=|candidate_i−y_i|`, `b_i=|frozen_primary_persistence_i−y_i|`, `d_i=a_i−b_i`; the candidate and persistence baseline must use the **same predeclared rate-evaluable row universe** and the all-registered denominator must separately report every missing/zero-game/ID mismatch/fallback. A negative paired mean difference indicates improved rate MAE, but is not itself season-total/draft utility. Let `B_(p,Y)=mean(b_i)>0`, `D_(p,Y)=mean(d_i)` and `R_(p,Y)=D_(p,Y)/B_(p,Y)` denote hypothetical relative position MAE regression. A future audited protocol would justify a **symbolic noninferiority margin** `m_p` and simultaneously require an upper confidence limit `U_p(R_(p,2028))≤m_p` for *all four* positions, **plus** separate pooled/season/tail/rank and source/fallback/integrity benefit gates. None of `m_p`, `U_p`, alpha, tail thresholds or model inputs is set by WR-113. The v2.1 historical `0.05` cap remains the **failed prior protocol's** criterion; it is not implicitly the new model's approved numeric margin.

**Cluster size and dependence.** Index preseason-eligible **independent player clusters**, not multiple rows as extra independent players. A future source/custody gate would report for each of QB/RB/WR/TE: `N_registered,p,Y`; `K_unique_id,p,Y`; position/ID transitions, shared teams/opponents/games; `K_evaluable,p,Y`; rate-undefined zero-game count; missing, attrition and permissible fallback; and uncertainty due to within-player repetition, roster/season shocks and unequal weights. For hypothetical per-cluster weights `w_i`, the illustrative Kish weighted `K_eff,p,Y=(Σ_i w_i)^2/Σ_i w_i^2` can signal loss from weight imbalance but **does not correct** player/team/game/season correlation. A subsequent independent statistician must justify the actual cluster/strata resampling model and small-cluster finite-sample behavior before any 2027 target access. Repeated players across 2027/2028 and a shared NFL season regime are not independent season replications just because their rows are distinct.

**Symbolic adequacy/power screen (entirely hypothetical).** If a position has `K_eff,p` truly independent clusters, pre-outcome planning values for paired-loss standard deviation `σ_p`, desired detectable *benefit* `δ_p>0`, one-sided allocated error `α_p` and desired power `1−β_p`, a crude normal-approximate screen is `K_needed,p ≳ [(z_(1−α_p)+z_(1−β_p))σ_p/δ_p]^2`. For a position-noninferiority claim, the relevant design distance is between the **prospectively hypothesized** `R_p` and `m_p`, while ratio-denominator uncertainty requires separate treatment. These algebraic placeholders neither choose numeric values nor imply actual QB/RB/WR/TE population size, attainable power, signal or likelihood of model success. A cluster-resampling or robust **predeclared** alternative is required when heavy tails, correlated teammates, few position clusters or positive target attrition invalidate the approximation. No football data, even public 2026 numbers, were consulted.
