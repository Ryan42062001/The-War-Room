# Superseded Role — Troubleshooting & Root Cause Engineer

The temporary Troubleshooting & Root Cause Engineer role is superseded by the permanent first-class:

**Work Helper / Super Troubleshooter / Cross-Functional Operator**

Canonical charter:
`.ai/roles/WORK_HELPER.md`

Persistent workspace:
`.ai/work_helper/`

Do not activate this legacy role for new work.

Existing historical task references to `Troubleshooting & Root Cause Engineer`, `temporary troubleshooter`, or `Temporary Troubleshooting` remain historical context only and should be interpreted under the rules that applied at the time.

For new troubleshooting/cross-layer escalation, Manager must create or assign a Work Helper task using the activation contract in `.ai/shared/WORKFLOW.md` and `.ai/roles/WORK_HELPER.md`.
