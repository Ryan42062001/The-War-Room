## 4. Three candidate directions for a later predeclared decision — no winner selected

1. **Persistence-only / minimal adjustment benchmark (CONTROL, not automatic improvement).** Predefine a `prev1_ppr_pg` persistence-only rate forecast and no model correction as a mandatory prospective challenger or abstention mode. **Rationale:** it is the accepted historical primary baseline, strong enough to defeat v2.1 RB in 2024–2025. **Falsification:** future proposed custom adjustment does not clear the pre-frozen MAE, uncertainty and position guards versus this control. **Caveat:** a zero-correction benchmark alone need not improve rank or draft utility and is not equivalent to current ECR value authority.
2. **Position-aware, train-only shrinkage with separately modeled availability (RESEARCH CANDIDATE).** A single preregistered architecture could use previous performance and pre-outcome usage/participation, estimate per-game residual correction with position-aware statistical partial pooling and *train-only* regularization/uncertainty, and keep games-played/availability as a distinct prediction target. **Rationale:** plausible lower variance and explicit rate-vs-exposure separation; does **not** prescribe RB-specific fixes after seeing its confirmation fail. **Falsification:** under future rolling locks, correction fails to beat persistence, per-position noninferiority or reliable games/role calibration, or trained controls have unacceptable fallback/missingness. **Open questions:** rights/time validity for participation fields, minimum per-position support, whether a new games target can be identified without selection bias. No model family or hyperparameters are selected in WR-110.
3. **Distributional / confidence-aware rate forecast with selective abstention (RESEARCH CANDIDATE).** Prospectively produce an uncertainty interval or predictive quantiles from predeclared eligible prior-only fields; calibrate on training/development information available **before** each future target; abstain to persistence or show “uncertain” where unsupported. **Rationale:** a single point MAE obscures uncertain RB roles, tail risk, and ranking instability; intervals could improve display honesty. **Falsification:** future independently evaluated interval coverage, width, rank/tail calibration or selective decision utility fails predeclared gates, or selection/abstention systematically hides weak positions. **Open questions:** coverage by position, player-cluster dependence, objective/cost of abstention and required data rights. Interval display is research-only, not a live recommendation mandate.

These are competing **design directions**, not three scored entrants and not an endorsement of any winner. Selecting an executable family, feature list, training-only tuning algorithm, fallback, source identity or adjustment weight is a later Manager/R&D protocol decision requiring independent audit **before future data exposure**. Do not run three candidates on 2024–2025 and choose by the known RB outcome. Any prospective multi-arm comparison requires a fixed finite family and prespecified multiple-comparison treatment or a single chosen primary arm before confirmation access.
