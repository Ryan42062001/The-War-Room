## 3. Statistical-power feasibility, symbolic only — no observed 2026 counts or fitted effects

The eventual independent statistician/Auditor must define the claim and error budgets *before* outcomes and estimate power **without selecting parameters from WR-101's known failed RB gate**. Neither historical row count nor an invented future cohort can prove a 2028 season adequate. Evaluate **all four** positions QB/RB/WR/TE; no post-hoc exemption for a weak/underpowered position.

Let `p ∈ {QB,RB,WR,TE}`, `Y ∈ {2027,2028}`, and `i` index preseason-locked individual player clusters with a defined rate target. Define paired per-player loss difference `d_(i,p,Y) = |candidate_i − target_i| − |baseline_i − target_i|` (negative = custom forecast better) and primary-baseline MAE `B_(p,Y) > 0` over an **identical, predeclared evaluable universe**. Let `r_(p,Y) = mean(d_(i,p,Y)) / B_(p,Y)` be relative position regression; a future noninferiority margin `m_p`, prespecified *before* new target exposure, requires a conservative simultaneous upper confidence bound for `r_(p,2028)` at or below `m_p`, **plus** separately predeclared pooled-benefit, season and tail/order checks. The former WR-095 `0.05` cap is an **historical model gate, not newly adopted by WR-112**; independent protocol review must justify any future position margin and simultaneous error control without adjusting it to known RB results. A noninferiority PASS alone does not prove overall improvement.

**Paired precision:** future independent design specifies player clustering and any teams/common-game/season correlation. For illustration only, if hypothetical `K_p` independent player clusters yield paired loss standard deviation `s_p`, a normal-approximate one-sided upper CI half-width behaves like `z_(1−α_p)·s_p/√K_p` (with `α_p` a **future** prespecified four-position familywise allocation). Hypothetical detectable beneficial difference `δ_p > 0` and desired power `1−β_p` imply a **planning** relation of the form `K_p,needed ≳ ((z_(1−α_p)+z_(1−β_p))·s_p/δ_p)^2` under idealized independent, near-normal clusters; for noninferiority against margin, replace `δ_p` by the hypothesized distance `m_p−r_p>0`, with a separate paired ratio-denominator uncertainty analysis. These are **symbolic feasibility formulas, not actual thresholds/power estimates, empirical RB assumptions or a model fit**. If the future target population has heavy tails, informative missingness or few players, an independent statistician must use a frozen clustered/bootstrap or robust design appropriate to that outcome rather than trust this approximation.

**Effective clusters, not nominal rows:** if a player has `w_i` eligible repeated observations or a future design applies unequal weights, an illustrative Kish count is `K_eff = (Σ_i w_i)^2 / Σ_i w_i^2` over genuinely independent player clusters; when `w_i=1`, `K_eff=K`. It **does not** correct shared game/opponent/team shocks, persistent correlated players, year effects or selection/dropout by itself. A design-effect sensitivity such as `n_eff≈n/[1+(m̄−1)ρ]` is only a **hypothetical** cluster approximation and cannot manufacture independently observed seasons. The audited power contract must state joint dependence, cluster unit, repeated-player linkage between 2027/2028, bootstrap resampling scope and a small-number-of-clusters failure gate.

**Illustrative fabricated scenario, not football evidence:** assume position X has `K_X=40` independent clusters and another position Z has `K_Z=8`; assume equal paired-loss variability and equal target improvement. Then the idealized normal CI width in Z would be `√(40/8)=√5` times the X width. This is **entirely invented arithmetic to show dependence of precision on support**; it is not any real QB/RB/WR/TE count, a proposed minimum, a pass probability, or grounds to omit Z. A future source gate must obtain permissioned **pre-outcome cohort-only** counts/ID profiles and conservative precision assumptions through a separately reviewed design path; data-derived estimates must not leak 2027/2028 target outcomes into threshold selection.

**Four-position simultaneity and pooled benefit:** predeclare a joint decision rule that controls familywise erroneous noninferiority across all four positions (e.g. α allocations and simultaneous cluster intervals), while also requiring independently demonstrated pooled candidate gain relative to same-row persistence and no unacceptable season/tail/rank regressions. Fix denominator-zero behavior, tie/weighting, multiple baselines, intended estimand versus rate-evaluable subset, resampling seed/replicates, finite-sample and imputation treatment before accessing target labels. Candidate, all baselines and designed fallbacks must be evaluated on matched target rows; no player or position may be dropped because its projection failed.

**Missingness/dropout:** register all prior-season-eligible returning-player predictions at preseason lock, then report (a) all registered, (b) played ≥1 recorded game, (c) zero-game and target undefined, (d) ID mismatch, (e) explicit, legitimate predeclared missing-source/forecast fallback. Compute attrition separately by position and model/baseline availability. If evaluation conditional on playing biases the decision claim or makes one position too sparse, report insufficient information; do not set zero games to zero PPR/game, condition membership on 2028 plays or infer that rates settle games-played/season-total value.

**Year-level sensitivity and 2028-only confirmation:** two distinct seasons give only one validation and one confirmation year, not a reliable estimate of multi-year environmental variance. A player-cluster bootstrap within 2028 can bound conditional player-sample uncertainty but **cannot** demonstrate robust inference across independent season regimes. A single 2028 confirmation might suffice for the **narrow, explicitly qualified two-observed-season claim** only if the future independent audit accepts position-effective support, simultaneous uncertainty, threshold/power assumptions, predeclared correction/dropout and allowable conditional scope. If year-level robustness/generalization requires multiple confirmation seasons or any position's precision/support is inadequate, Manager must choose a *different pre-outcome* contract (possibly adding a future 2029+ season) **before evaluating 2027/2028 outcomes**. Do not add/drop future seasons or weaken the position margin after seeing a fail. An unavailable 2028 target cannot be treated as a confirmation PASS.
