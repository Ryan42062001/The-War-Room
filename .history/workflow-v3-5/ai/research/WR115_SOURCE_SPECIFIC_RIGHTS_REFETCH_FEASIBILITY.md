## 4. Distinct failure modes, even if a release appears immutable

- **Immutable != persistent:** opt-in release assets may be locked while a release exists, but the entire release/repository can cease to be available; no publisher promise of retention through 2027 validation, 2028 confirmation and subsequent independent review is established. Generic GitHub APIs may be public without guaranteeing future availability.
- **Hash != retrievable evidence:** a recorded digest without lawful surviving exact bytes and legally entitled independently acquired reference gives identity evidence **only for an object if later found**; it cannot reproduce source fields/IDs/game denominator and all QB/RB/WR/TE scoring evidence.
- **Reissue != same version:** corrected same-path objects, loader cache changes, format conversions (`rds` vs `csv` vs `parquet`), regenerated archives and differently serialized copies may be semantically similar but **not exact bytes**. Do not equate a season/year and URL with an immutable object. An independently verified *hash-identical lawful copy* fits Ryan's stated objective; a merely equivalent reconstructed dataset does not.
- **Rights != access or storage:** public CC BY may grant reproduction for actual licensed material without a private contract, but separate upstream owner/recipient/account terms or lack of source hosting may still defeat lawful independent re-fetch. Auditor temporary raw working files/cache/backups must be licensed and lawfully deleted; no local raw retention by Ryan does not confer unrestricted auditor raw retention or unrestricted keyed derived outputs.
- **Corrections and planned closure:** a T27 or T28 target may need a separately predeclared correction-closure version. If corrected bytes differ from an earlier target snapshot, both the selected version identity and correction lineage must be documented; retrospective source replacement after seeing validation/confirmation metrics is forbidden.
- **Access-window mismatch:** rights valid at an authorized pre-`T27` feature intake do not alone prove a later independently entitled reviewer can access that exact asset at both the post-2027 and post-2028 review windows, including after provider policy changes. If any necessary role is missing then the full two-season reference-level claim fails.
