## 1. Minimum future source product and purpose

The *conditional minimal path* is a separately rights-reviewed future instance of **nflverse-data `stats_player` regular-season player summary** (documented `nflreadr::load_player_stats(..., summary_level="reg")`), not a mixture of mutable current metadata, game-by-game future inputs, proprietary draft capital or ESPN/FantasyPros rankings. It would supply (A) prior completed season `Y-1` rows **available as of a frozen pre-Y cutoff** for the returning-player cohort/approved feature schema and (B) separately versioned, post-Y-regular-season player summary target rows to evaluate the frozen, prior-locked preseason full-PPR-per-recorded-game predictions for target Y. For 2027, this means a separately accepted **preseason 2027 as-of 2026 regular-season prior-feature instance** and a different **postseason 2027 target instance**. For 2028, analogously pre-2028 prior-feature and post-2028 target instances. Each year's feature/target byte set and license/producer identity is a **distinct** prospective source-instance decision, not a broad four-file download approval or a 2014–2025 legacy asset reuse.

Only rights-authorized, predeclared player ID/season/position/games and passing/rushing/receiving regular-season source columns necessary to reproduce the *future separately audited* schema and full-PPR numerator/recorded-game denominator may enter a future model contract. Exact field names, completeness and compatibility are **UNKNOWN** until a later, independently reviewed source-instance gate. Zero games must not be imputed as a zero rate or silently removed after seeing targets; the future protocol must separately lock the denominator/availability and missing-target population treatment. NFLverse `players` metadata, FTN participation, `draft_picks`, commercial rankings/ADP, actual 2026 outcomes or current-year target fields are **not admitted by WR-112**.
