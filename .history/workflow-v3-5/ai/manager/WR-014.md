## Accepted core findings
- A War Room-owned shadow projection/value model is technically feasible enough to justify a bounded non-production experiment.
- Do not predict draft rank directly; forecast per-game Full-PPR production, opportunity/role, availability where defensible, season distribution, then apply league-specific replacement value downstream.
- Initial experimental architecture should be position-specific QB/RB/WR/TE models, with a transparent baseline and a higher-capacity challenger.
- PFF is excluded under current ordinary-access terms.
- NFL Next Gen Stats should be excluded from the initial rights-conservative experiment unless explicit rights are established.
- nflverse documents a post-2024 injury-data gap, so current availability modeling remains incomplete from that source alone.
- No lawful/complete historical contemporaneous FantasyPros ECR corpus was established for direct retrospective comparison.
- No production authority change is justified without rolling held-out and prospective evidence.
