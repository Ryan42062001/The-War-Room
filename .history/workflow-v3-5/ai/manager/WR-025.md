## Objective
Use rights-clean historical football data to identify which preseason-known player characteristics consistently predict strong fantasy outcomes, upside/breakout outcomes, and downside/bust risk, and build a research-only historical ranking prototype from those signals.

The purpose is to answer the user's practical question: what statistics/context tend to identify players who should rank higher, and what warning signals tend to identify players likely to underperform?

WR-021 established a promising returning-player PPR/game signal, but it was designed as a projection experiment rather than an interpretable ranking/risk research program. The user now explicitly wants historical evidence for ranking drivers and bust warnings.

This is a legitimate maintenance-mode reactivation trigger: explicit new product/research requirement.

## Research questions
At minimum investigate:

1. Which preseason-known features are consistently associated with better next-season Full-PPR performance by position?
2. Which features predict positive residuals versus a transparent preseason baseline (breakout/upside)?
3. Which features predict negative residuals versus that baseline (bust/downside risk)?
4. Which signals are stable across seasons and positions versus one-off correlations?
5. Can an interpretable risk-adjusted ranking prototype beat a transparent historical baseline on rolling-origin evaluation?
6. Which signals should be treated as warnings rather than ranking boosts?
7. Are rookie and returning-player signals materially different?
8. Is a lawful historical draft-cost/ADP/ECR benchmark available? If not, explicitly distinguish performance-underperformance from true draft-cost 'bust' analysis.

## Terminology discipline
Do not claim causation from observational data.

Use language such as:
- predictive indicator
- historical association
- stable signal
- warning signal
- positive/negative residual

Do not state that a statistic "causes" a breakout or bust without causal evidence.

## Historical cohort / leakage controls
Use preseason-defined cohorts wherever defensible.

For target season Y:
- features must come from Y-1 or earlier completed seasons plus immutable/preseason-known context;
- no target-Y Week 1+ field may enter features;
- target outcomes are joined only for scoring/labels;
- zero/non-participation should remain visible for availability/downside analysis rather than silently disappearing.

Because 2022–2025 outcomes have already been seen by the project, do not represent them as pristine holdouts.

Use rolling-origin / expanding-window evaluation and report per-season stability.

## Baselines / targets
At minimum preserve a transparent returning-player baseline such as previous-season Full-PPR PPR/game.

For drafted rookies/no-history players, use a transparent position + draft-capital prior if included.

Primary performance outcomes should include:
- next-season Full-PPR points per recorded game;
- recorded games / availability proxy;
- season-total Full-PPR where defensible.

Breakout/upside and bust/downside should primarily be defined as residuals versus a predeclared preseason expectation/baseline, not hindsight rank thresholds chosen after seeing results.

If a lawful historical draft-cost benchmark is found, add a clearly separate draft-cost-relative analysis. If none is found, state that 'bust' means performance-under-expectation, not fantasy-draft-cost bust.

## Signal analysis
Use interpretable methods and stability checks. At minimum:
- standardized regularized-model coefficients or equivalent interpretable effects;
- permutation importance or similarly leakage-safe model importance for one nonlinear challenger if justified;
- per-position signal direction and stability across seasons;
- simple threshold/bucket analyses for important continuous variables where useful;
- correlation/importance uncertainty or resampling where practical;
- multicollinearity caution for closely related usage/production variables.

Potential feature families, only if rights-cleared and point-in-time:
- prior PPR/game and trend;
- targets/carries/receptions/attempts per game;
- target share / air-yard share / WOPR;
- efficiency / EPA-derived prior-season summaries;
- prior games / availability;
- age;
- experience;
- draft capital;
- rookie indicator;
- team change if historically as-of semantics are defensible;
- multi-year trend / recency;
- missing-history indicators.

## Ranking prototype
Build at most one primary interpretable historical ranking prototype plus one diagnostic challenger.

The prototype may combine:
- projected performance;
- availability/downside risk;
- upside residual probability or expected residual;

but its formula/weights must be predeclared before final scoring and remain research-only.

Evaluate ranking quality using chronological out-of-sample seasons with metrics such as:
- MAE/RMSE for PPR/game;
- Spearman;
- rank MAE;
- top-N overlap by position;
- downside/bust precision/recall or calibration if a classification label is defined;
- upside/breakout precision/recall or calibration if defined;
- position-level and season-level stability.
