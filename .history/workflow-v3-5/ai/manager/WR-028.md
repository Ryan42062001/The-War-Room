## Architecture principle
Keep independent layers rather than one opaque score:
1. point-in-time data and provenance;
2. expected performance;
3. opportunity / role;
4. availability;
5. downside / breakout / uncertainty warnings;
6. rookie handling;
7. season-total projection;
8. positional replacement value;
9. cross-position draft value;
10. tiers / confidence / explanations;
11. market timing and live draft strategy as separate downstream signals.

ESPN ADP/rank does not enter intrinsic player value under WR-D001.

## Completion states
### Research-complete
Historical methods, inputs, rights, point-in-time semantics, validation and failure modes are sufficient to select a fixed engine architecture.

### Engine-complete
A reproducible engine generates QB/RB/WR/TE preseason expected PPR/game, availability, season totals, risk/warning outputs, replacement-adjusted values, overall/position ranks, tiers, confidence and explanations from cutoff-safe inputs.

### Shadow-ready
The engine is integrated non-authoritatively with deterministic versioning, provenance, fail-closed data handling, rollback and independent QA while FantasyPros remains authority.

### Production-candidate
Historical architecture gates plus the frozen WR-023 prospective gate are satisfied and Manager separately authorizes a production-ranking milestone.

### Production-authoritative
Requires a later durable Manager decision, Builder implementation, independent audit and explicit WR-D001 replacement/amendment.

## Cross-cutting engine contracts — REQUIRED
These apply across later phases and must be resolved before `ENGINE-COMPLETE`/`SHADOW-READY`.

### Point-in-time feature store
Every feature must have stable entity keys, observation/event time, available-at/publication time when knowable, season/cutoff semantics, revision policy and joins that reject future-visible records.

### Immutable data/provenance manifests
Generated boards/models must record source/release identity, retrieval time, SHA-256 where possible, upstream/provider, license/right evidence, schema version/hash, row/date coverage, derivation-code version and feature-artifact hash.

### Missing-data and fallback policy
Define required vs optional feature families, training-only imputation, missing indicators, fallback model selection, candidate-rejection thresholds and fail-closed rules. Missing context must never silently become zero.

### Coverage / confidence
Emit per-player and per-feature-family coverage, provenance quality, observation age and confidence caveats. Low coverage may reduce confidence or force fallback even when a numeric prediction exists.

### Prediction uncertainty
Calibrate uncertainty separately for performance, availability, rookies and season totals. Evaluate empirical coverage and sharpness; do not present uncalibrated probability-like labels as certainty.

### Ranking sensitivity
Measure rank movement under input perturbation, bootstrap/model uncertainty and source omission. Adjacent ordering that is not stable should be represented through tiers/confidence rather than false precision.

### Tier methodology
Generate tiers from meaningful value gaps relative to uncertainty/sensitivity with deterministic tie rules. Avoid arbitrary equal-size tier buckets.

### League replacement/value contract
Formalize league size, starters, scoring, FLEX eligibility/demand allocation, roster/bench assumptions, waiver/replacement definition and sensitivity scenarios. Football projection is league-agnostic; draft value is league-specific.

### K / DST policy
Do not force K/DST through the QB/RB/WR/TE engine. A later phase must explicitly choose dedicated simple models, retained external authority, or separate endgame treatment before a complete production board is claimed.

### Late entrants and player-universe policy
Define UDFAs, undrafted rookies, free agents, reinstatements, late signings, supplemental players, cohort-entry cutoff, identity matching, fallback prior and uncertainty.

### Position changes / eligibility
Define canonical preseason football position, platform/league eligibility, multi-position handling, historical mapping and which eligibility controls replacement value.

### Versioning
Version independently: raw data snapshot, feature schema, training code, fitted model parameters, risk calibration, value transformation, league config and generated board.

### Champion / Challenger governance
Challengers remain isolated from production. Predeclare evaluation gates, final confirmation folds, multiple-testing/selection controls where material, evaluation owner and Manager-only promotion authority.

### Last-Known-Good rollback
Board/model publication must be atomic. Preserve prior compatible artifacts and define rollback trigger, owner and compatibility checks. Source or model refresh failure must not silently overwrite the LKG board.

### Refresh cadence
Define annual retraining cutoff, post-NFL-draft refresh, roster/depth updates, final preseason refresh/freeze, and what data may update after fantasy drafts begin without retraining the learned model.

### Source failure handling
Per feature family define stale thresholds, retry rules, optional-family degradation, required-family fail-close and alerts. Prohibit partial mixed-snapshot publication.

### Drift monitoring
Monitor source/schema/null/coverage/identity/distribution drift plus model residual/calibration/rank-turnover drift after outcomes become legally available for monitoring.

### Explainability
Player output should identify major driver families, warnings, sensitivity/coverage caveats, model version and as-of timestamp without implying causation.

### FantasyPros comparison boundary
FantasyPros may be an external benchmark only when lawful and methodologically valid. It does not become a training input or intrinsic feature without a new approved hybrid architecture.

### Intrinsic value vs live strategy
Maintain separate artifacts/APIs. Intrinsic custom board answers player value; live recommendation layer adds ESPN survival/ADP, roster needs, drafted state, opponent context and draft timing.

### Reproducibility / audit
Require deterministic seeds where applicable, dependency lock/runtime metadata, frozen fold definitions, golden test datasets, identity reconciliation and independent leakage/provenance audit before shadow integration.

### Phase 3 — Rookie engine v1 — PLANNED

### Phase 4 — Availability / expected-games model — PLANNED
Predict recorded games separately from PPR/game. Do not call it medical/injury prediction without admitted medical/injury evidence.

### Phase 5 — Season-total distribution — PLANNED
Combine expected performance and games into expected season PPR plus calibrated downside/median/upside ranges where defensible.

### Phase 6 — Replacement value / cross-position custom draft value — PLANNED
Apply league replacement contract, FLEX, scarcity and value-above-replacement. Market timing remains separate.

### Phase 7 — Historical complete-engine replay — PLANNED
Replay prior preseasons and evaluate overall/position rank, top-N, VORP/value error, tiers, warnings, catastrophic over-ranks, stability and draft simulations only when assumptions are predeclared.

### Phase 8 — 2026 custom development board — PLANNED
Generate a full 2026 board from frozen/preseason inputs. Because architecture is being developed post-kickoff, label it DEVELOPMENT / RETROSPECTIVE-HISTORICAL, not a new pristine prospective test.

### Phase 9 — Shadow production integration — PLANNED
Builder integration only after Manager authorization. FantasyPros remains authoritative. Require deterministic version/hash, fail-closed ingestion, provenance, LKG rollback and no silent recommendation influence.

### Phase 10 — Independent engine QA — PLANNED
Audit rights/provenance, PIT leakage, reproducibility, value transforms, replacement logic, rookies, uncertainty/warnings, fallback/rollback and production-authority isolation.

### Phase 11 — 2026 prospective validation — FROZEN / EVENT-DRIVEN
WR-023 remains authoritative. Optional Week 4/8/13 descriptive checkpoints; completed Week 18 regular season is decisive. This tests the frozen WR-021 model, not post-kickoff revisions.

### Phase 12 — Production-ranking promotion decision — FUTURE / CONDITIONAL
Only after engine/audit gates and WR-023 final gate. Possible outcomes: keep FantasyPros authority with custom secondary signal; approved blend; replace authority; or remain shadow-only. Any production change requires a new durable decision and implementation/audit milestone.
